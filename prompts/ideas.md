## Your task
Suggest {count} video ideas. For each one:
- `title`: the YouTube title, as a curiosity question or a surprising claim, at most
  90 characters.
- `hook`: the first line the viewer hears — it must stop the scroll in two seconds.
- `brief`: 2-4 sentences: the angle, the 3-4 beats the video walks through, and the
  surprising fact it ends on.
- `why_now`: one line on why it is timely — the trend item above it comes from, or
  "Evergreen" when it is not tied to one.
- `sources`: the URLs of the trend items it came from, copied from the list above
  ([] when evergreen).

## Rules
1. Every idea is a different subject: never two ideas about the same thing.
2. Favour concrete, visual, surprising "how does this really work" questions that
   animation can show. A timely idea beats an evergreen one of the same quality.
3. Accurate and widely accepted facts only — no myths, no made-up numbers.
4. Skip tragedies, violence, politics, celebrities and private people; nothing
   sexual and nothing dangerous to copy.
5. Mix them: mostly ideas that are easy to animate, a few more ambitious ones.
