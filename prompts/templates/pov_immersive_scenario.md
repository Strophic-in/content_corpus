## Template: POV / Immersive Scenario
"POV: …" hook → scenario → escalating problem → outcome. Shot 1 (`role` "pov_hook") has `text`
starting "POV:" (at most 10 words), and every generated `visual` is first-person: the camera is
the viewer's eyes, their hands may enter the frame, the world comes at them. Each later shot's
`text` is the next beat in a few words (or "" when the picture says it). Escalate shot by shot;
the last shot is the outcome or the twist. Anything fictional or speculative that a viewer could
take for real carries `label` "Fiction" or "Hypothetical" on the first shot.
