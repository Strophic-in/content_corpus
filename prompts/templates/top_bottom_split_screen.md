## Template: Top/Bottom Split Screen
Two stacked panels, both with a purpose. The top half shows the subject or the action — only the
middle of each shot's picture is seen, so frame the subject centrally. The bottom half shows the
words: the captions of the narration, with the shot's `text` (a short headline, at most 8 words)
above them. To show a second picture in the bottom half instead (a diagram, the result, the
other side), set `panel_asset` to a picture's number from the footage list. Hook → demonstration
→ explanation → payoff. A shot may set `layout` "full" for a moment that needs the whole screen.
