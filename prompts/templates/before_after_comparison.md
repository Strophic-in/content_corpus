## Template: Before vs. After Comparison
Initial state → changed state → explanation of what changed. The "before" shot has `label`
"Before", the "after" shot `label` "After" and `transition` "wipe"; describe the same framing in
both so the comparison is fair. Then "explanation" shots say what changed and why. To show both
at once, a shot may use `layout` "split" with the before picture as `panel_asset`. If either side
is an illustration or simulation rather than a real result, say so in `text`.

For news (a monument burned, a town flooded, a coastline eaten away): open on the place as it
was - a real picture marked BEFORE in the footage list, labelled "Before" - then wipe to the
event itself, labelled "Now", from the event's own pictures or clips. Same place, matching
framing where you can. Then what happened, why it matters and what is not known yet. If there
is no BEFORE picture, a generated before shot says so in `text` ("Illustration"), never passes
as a photograph.
