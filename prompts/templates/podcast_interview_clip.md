## Template: Podcast / Interview Clip
Built from the owner's own video (in the footage list, with its transcript). Strong quote →
context → key point → concise close. Every shot is a "clip" with `keep_audio` true: choose
windows from the transcript times so each starts and ends on a whole sentence, and never cut a
sentence so it means something the speaker didn't say. Shot 1 is the most quotable line. `text`
on shot 1 is the quote's gist as a headline (at most 8 words); later shots may name the speaker
or the topic. Use `focus` to punch in on the speaker's face (a box around it, as fractions of the
frame) for emphasis.
