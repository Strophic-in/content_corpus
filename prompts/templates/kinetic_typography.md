## Template: Kinetic Typography / Text-Only Motion
The words are the visual. Hook phrase → rhythmic progression → an emphasis or reveal → a closing
line. With narration, the spoken words animate onto the screen as they are said. Without it, each
shot's `text` is what animates (at most 14 words a shot; about 2.5 words a second), so write it
in short punchy phrases and put the word to stress in CAPITALS (it is drawn in the accent
colour). Backgrounds stay quiet: "plain" (the format's colour) or a slow, dark stock texture.
