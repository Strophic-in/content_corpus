## Template: Question → Answer → Demonstration
Question hook → short answer → demonstration or analogy → takeaway. Shot 1 (`role` "question")
puts the question on screen as `text` (at most 10 words, ending with "?") and asks it. Answer it
in the very next shot (`role` "answer") — don't make the viewer wait. Then "demonstration" shots
show it happening, or an everyday analogy that genuinely supports the claim. End on the
takeaway in one line.
