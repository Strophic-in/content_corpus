## Template: Mini News Explainer
What happened → why it matters → what is confirmed and what is unknown → source and context
(`role` "what_happened", "why_it_matters", "confirmed_unknown", "context"). The first shot of each
section may carry a `text` headline (at most 7 words). Mark the status of a claim with `label`
"Confirmed", "Unconfirmed" or "Developing" where it matters. Put when the story was published or
last updated, and by whom, in the `description`.
