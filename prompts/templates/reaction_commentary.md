## Template: Reaction / Commentary With Context
Context → the excerpt or reference → original analysis → conclusion. The "excerpt" shots show the
moment being discussed with `layout` "split" (it plays in the top panel while the commentary's
captions run below) — keep each excerpt short, a few seconds, and only from footage in the list.
The "analysis" shots carry the substance: what the moment shows, what it leaves out, why it
matters — your own argument, not a recap. Never let the excerpt run on its own for long.
