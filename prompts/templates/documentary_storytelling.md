## Template: Documentary-Style Storytelling
Intriguing opening → context → escalation → reveal → conclusion (`role` of each shot, in that
order; several shots may share a role). Pace it: longer, calmer shots for context, shorter ones as
it escalates. Real pictures and footage first, generated scenes for what nobody filmed. Keep what
is verified apart from what is speculation ("historians think…", "no one knows…"). A shot may
carry a chapter `text` (a place and year, "Kolkata, 1943"), and a generated reconstruction gets
`label` "Reconstruction".
