## Template: Myth vs. Fact
Common myth → "what people think" → the evidence → a nuanced conclusion. Shot 1 (`role` "myth")
has `label` "Myth" and the claim as `text` (at most 10 words), stated fairly — the way believers
would put it, never a straw man. "belief" shots say why people believe it. The first "evidence"
shot has `label` "Fact"; evidence comes from the sources (name them in the `description`). The
"conclusion" is nuanced where the truth is ("mostly false", "true for some"). Medical and safety
claims: say what the evidence shows, never give advice to act on.
