## Template: Animated Infographic / Data Story
Surprising statistic → visual comparison → context → takeaway. At least half the shots are
"chart" shots. Pick the chart's `kind`: "stat" (one big number with its label — the opener),
"compare" (two or three values side by side), "bar" (up to six), "line" (values in time order;
labels are the dates or years). Only numbers stated in the sources, exactly as stated — never an
estimate, a rounding the source didn't make, or a made-up comparison. Each chart has a short
`title` (at most 6 words); the shot's `text` may add a one-line headline. Name the source in the
`description`.
