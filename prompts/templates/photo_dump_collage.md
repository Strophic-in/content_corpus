## Template: Photo Dump / Collage Carousel
Strong cover → varied photos → a detail or context slide → a final reveal or summary. Almost
every slide's `visual` is a picture from the footage list (`source` "asset" and its number), each
used once, ordered for rhythm: wide, close, wide; colourful, calm. `heading` is a short caption
(at most 6 words, or "") and `body` is "" on photo slides; the detail slide may carry up to 25
words. The cover's `heading` names the collection.
