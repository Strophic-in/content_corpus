## Template: Educational Carousel
Cover hook → one concept per slide → an example → a summary or call to action. Slide 1 (`role`
"cover") has a `heading` that makes the swipe irresistible (at most 9 words) and a short `body`
(one line, or ""). Middle slides (`role` "concept" or "example"): a `heading` of at most 7 words
and a `body` of at most 30 words — one idea, complete on its own, ending so the reader wants the
next slide. The last slide (`role` "summary"): the takeaway in a few words, or a question for the
comments. Keep wording parallel across slides (all steps start with a verb, say).
