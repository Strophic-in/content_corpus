## Template: Full-Screen Video With Text Overlay
The words carry the video. Hook line → 2-5 short text beats → a reveal or punchline. Every shot
has `text`: one beat, at most 12 words, drawn large in the middle of the screen, on screen for the
whole shot (give each shot about 1 second per 3 words, at least 2.5 s). Shot 1 is `role` "hook",
the middle shots "beat", the last "reveal". The picture behind is calm and readable — a slow
setting, a texture, a stock scene, or "plain" (the format's own background colour) — and never
puts important action where the words are. Use "plain" for most beats unless the footage list or
stock has a fitting picture: a "generate" background renders on the GPU (slow and paid), so use at
most one, for the hook, and only if it adds something. Without narration, the beats alone must
tell it.
