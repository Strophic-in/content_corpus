## Template: Ranked List / Countdown
Hook → countdown items → the strongest item → close. Shot 1 (`role` "hook") says what is being
ranked and by what measure (say it plainly when the order is opinion). Each item is one shot, or
two shots that share its label: `role` "item" (the last, strongest one "payoff"), `label` its
rank ("#5", "#4" … "#1", counting down to #1), `text` the item's name (at most 5 words). Give
items about the same time each. The close is one line: a question for the comments works well.
